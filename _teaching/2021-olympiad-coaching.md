I coach students preparing for international mathematics competitions,
including TIMO, HKIMO, SASMO, SEAMO, AMC and ASMO.

Sessions focus on problem-solving strategy rather than formula recall:
recognising problem types, choosing an approach before computing, and writing
solutions that a grader can follow. Areas covered include number theory,
combinatorics, geometry and functional equations at contest level.
