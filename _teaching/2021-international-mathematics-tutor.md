Since 2021 I have provided individualized mathematics instruction to students
following the IGCSE, GCSE, IB and A-Level curricula.

The work is mostly diagnostic: finding the exact step where a student's
reasoning breaks, and rebuilding from there rather than drilling past papers.
Topics span algebra, calculus, probability and statistics, and mechanics,
including Further Mathematics and IB HL material.

Teaching this range has been unexpectedly useful for research. Explaining why a
definition is shaped the way it is — repeatedly, to people who have not agreed
to accept it yet — is a good test of whether you understand it.
