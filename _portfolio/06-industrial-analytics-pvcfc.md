**Role:** Data Scientist Intern, PVCFC – PetroVietnam · **Oct 2024 – Apr 2025**

### What I did

* Extracted and cleaned operational and sales data from ERP systems, improving
  data quality and consistency across reporting.
* Built Power BI dashboards for revenue, production and sales monitoring.
* The dashboards supported data-driven planning decisions and contributed to a
  reduction of excess inventory of approximately 5%.

### Why it is here

Research code and production data have different failure modes. This internship
is where I learned how much of a modelling result depends on the data pipeline
underneath it — a habit I have kept in my experimental work.

**Tools:** SQL, Python, Power BI, ERP data extracts.
